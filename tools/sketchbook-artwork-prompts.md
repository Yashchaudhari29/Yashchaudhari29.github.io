# Sketchbook artwork prompts

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. Eight separate original landscape illustrations, served locally as WebP from assets/sketchbook/. These are conceptual visual metaphors, not technical diagrams.

## Shared direction

Landscape 3:2 premium editorial watercolor and fine ink illustration for an AI engineer's interactive sketchbook. Full-bleed flat artwork on warm ivory cotton paper; no photographed book, outer edges or center crease (the website supplies the book). Extremely fine sepia penwork, sage, teal and ochre washes, pencil construction lines, visible paper grain, a detailed panoramic composition with breathing room. No text, letters, logos, UI, watermark or neon.

## Subjects

- **attention.webp:** A brass telescope observatory and celestial garden, connected by bridges and constellation threads: a visual metaphor for attention.
- **retrieval.webp:** A vast old botanical research library, open drawers of specimen cards, ladders, shelves and delicate threads connecting a small reading desk to selected volumes, visual metaphor for retrieval-augmented generation.
- **diffusion.webp:** An exquisite kingfisher and its leafy riverside habitat gradually emerging from loose watercolor droplets and graphite dust on the left into precise fine penwork on the right, visual metaphor for diffusion and denoising.
- **experts.webp:** An elaborate architectural cutaway of interconnected specialist workshops under glass domes, an intricate railway switching station routes tiny lines of light to two workshops while others rest, visual metaphor for mixture of experts.
- **embeddings.webp:** A hand drawn celestial botanical atlas: plants, birds and seashell specimens arranged as small islands on a star map, fine curved contour lines and clusters of kindred forms, visual metaphor for embedding spaces.
- **lora.webp:** A beautiful antique mechanical orrery with large stable brass rings and two small emerald adjustment gears highlighted, intricate exploded engineering sketch, visual metaphor for low-rank adaptation.
- **vision.webp:** A field naturalist's study of a heron reflected in still water, delicate translucent optical frames and a series of small inset eye/lens studies around it, visual metaphor for computer vision.
- **agents.webp:** A winding miniature garden with branching paths, a small drafting desk, compass, clock and notebooks connected by a dotted walking route, visual metaphor for planning and tool-using agents.
